Version 1.11.0 helps us improve Sawari for you. The website and the simulator now count how drives go, so we can see where people get stuck and fix it.

What we learn

  • Which routes people finish and where they give up, so we can find the parts that are too hard or badly explained.
  • How long each route takes to load and whether a phone or computer struggles to keep up, so we can make Sawari smoother on more devices.
  • Which mistakes are most common, which helps us choose what to write about in the blog.
  • Which guides lead people to try a drive, and how scores and challenges get shared.

What stays private

  • Your settings and your best scores are still saved only in your browser, not on our servers.
  • No account, no sign-up, and nothing you type, such as the name on your score card, is collected.
  • We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to count visits and drives and to see how pages are used. The About and FAQ page says so too.

Nothing else changes

Driving, the routes, the score card and challenges all work as before. Take a drive.
