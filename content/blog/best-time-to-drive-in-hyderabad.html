The best time to drive in Hyderabad is early morning, before about 8 am, and weekend mornings are the calmest of all; the worst times are weekday peaks, roughly 8.30 to 11 am and 5 to 9 pm, plus heavy rain and big festival days. The right answer also depends on where you are going, so this guide breaks it down by time, day, area and season.

Timings here come from Hyderabad and Cyberabad traffic police advisories and local news reports, as of November 2026. Traffic changes with road works and events, so always check a live traffic app and the latest Hyderabad Traffic Police advisories before a long or important trip.

What is the best time of day to drive?

Early morning is best, mid-afternoon is a reasonable second choice, and the two rush hours are the times to avoid.

Treat them as a sound guide for the city's busy corridors in general. If you drive at night, read our night driving tips first.

Which days are easiest?

Sunday morning is usually the easiest time of the whole week, and Saturday morning is close behind. Many offices are closed at weekends, so the commuter peaks largely disappear.

  • Weekend mornings are the calmest, ideal for practice.
  • Weekend evenings are busy around malls, Hussain Sagar, Necklace Road, temples and restaurant areas.
  • Monday mornings and Friday evenings are often heavier than midweek, as people travel in and out of the city.
  • Days before long weekends and festivals bring heavy traffic on roads out of the city and towards bus and railway stations.

When should you drive in each area?

Each part of the city has its own rhythm, so plan by destination, not only by the clock.

IT corridor: HITEC City, Madhapur and Gachibowli

Go early in the morning or in the middle of the day. Office start and finish times vary, so the evening rush here is long and can run late into the night. Cyberabad traffic police have repeatedly asked companies to stagger log-out times, especially on heavy rain days. Our guide to Hyderabad traffic hotspots and peak hours names the usual bottlenecks.

Old City around the Charminar

Go early in the morning, before the shops open. Late mornings, evenings, weekends and festival days are much busier, with dense crowds on foot. During Ramzan the area stays busy late into the night: in 2026 the state government allowed shops to stay open until 5 am during the month. See our Charminar and Old City driving guide.

Secunderabad

Avoid the peaks around Paradise Circle and the railway station, which jam badly on wet days. Elevated corridor works between Bowenpally and Paradise have caused long delays, so check diversions before you set off.

To the airport

Plan backwards from your flight. The airport recommends arriving 120 minutes before a domestic flight and 180 minutes before an international one, so add your driving time and a generous margin if your trip crosses a rush hour. Late-night and very early trips on the PVNR Expressway or the Outer Ring Road are usually quick. Our guide to driving to Hyderabad airport covers routes and drop-off rules.

How do seasons and festivals change things?

Weather and festivals can make a normally easy drive slow, so check the calendar as well as the clock.

Monsoon

The south-west monsoon usually brings rain from about June to September, and heavy showers can continue into October. A downpour floods low junctions and underpasses and can double journey times. If heavy rain is forecast, travel before it starts or wait until the water clears. Our monsoon driving guide has the details.

Summer heat

From about March to May, afternoons are very hot. Driving in the early morning or after sunset is more comfortable for you and easier on the car, and it is worth keeping water in the car and the tyres properly inflated.

Festival periods

  • Ganesh festival and immersion. Immersion processions head to Hussain Sagar, and Tank Bund, Necklace Road, NTR Marg and nearby roads are closed or restricted, sometimes for more than a day. In 2026 the main immersions fell in late September.
  • Bonalu. During Ashada month, roughly July and August, police set diversions around the big temple celebrations at Golconda, Ujjaini Mahankali in Secunderabad and Lal Darwaza in the Old City.
  • Ramzan. Evenings and nights around the Charminar and the Old City markets are very crowded, with extra restrictions for Friday prayers and Eid.
  • New Year's Eve. On the night of 31 December 2025 police closed most flyovers except Begumpet and Tolichowki, and closed Tank Bund and Necklace Road to vehicles from 11 pm to 2 am. In past years the Outer Ring Road has also been closed to cars overnight. Expect drink-driving checks everywhere.
  • Sankranti and other long holidays. Roads out of the city and towards bus and railway stations are very busy just before the holiday.
Before any festival day, check the advisories from Hyderabad Traffic Police, Cyberabad Police and Rachakonda Police. They usually list closed roads, diversions and parking places a day or two in advance. The Hyderabad traffic helpline quoted in 2026 advisories is 9010203626.

What is the best time for learners?

Early on a weekend morning, in dry weather and daylight, on a route you already know. That combination gives you light traffic, good visibility and nothing new to process except the driving itself.

  1. Start with weekend mornings on wide, steady roads such as Tank Bund Road or Necklace Road.
  2. Move to weekday mid-mornings or early afternoons once you are steady.
  3. Try the edge of a rush hour on a familiar route.
  4. Leave full peak hours, night driving, heavy rain and festival days until last.

Practising at any hour with Sawari

Sawari is a free browser driving simulator on five real Hyderabad routes, from Tank Bund Road and Necklace Road to the Charminar and Pathergatti. You can pick the time of day and how heavy the traffic is, so you can rehearse a busy route in dense traffic before you face it at rush hour. It runs on phones held sideways, tablets, laptops and PCs, and the debrief lists every fault at the end.

It has no rain and cannot predict a real jam, and it is practice only, not a substitute for lessons. Try Cyber Towers to Jubilee Hills in dense traffic to get a feel for the IT corridor.
