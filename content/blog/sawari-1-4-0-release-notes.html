Version 1.4.0 is a clean-up of every route: we went over all five drives looking for anything that looked or felt wrong on the road, and fixed it. It also brings the website's light and dark themes to the simulator's start page.

A car that sounds like a car

  • A new engine sound modelled on a 1.2 litre four-cylinder: a slightly uneven idle, a deeper, rougher note under full throttle, a softer one when you lift off, and a short dip at each gear change.
  • Brakes you can hear: the pads hiss harder the more you press, tyres howl and the ABS chatters in a hard stop, and the car creaks and settles as it comes to rest.
  • Tyres scrub as you push through a tight bend and squeal once you go past their grip, with a pitch that wanders like real rubber. The handbrake makes them scrub too.
  • Wind noise at speed, a clunk when you engage reverse, and a slightly quieter engine from the driver's seat.

Smoother, cleaner roads

  • No more patches of ground poking through the road surface, and no pale patches that looked like dirt.
  • Where two roads meet at the same height they no longer flicker into each other, and sharp bends have no gaps at the corner.
  • Side roads now slope gently into the main road instead of dropping or showing a dark wall along their edge.
  • Zebra crossings and stop lines stop at the edge of a junction instead of painting across it.
  • Footpaths and kerb strips no longer reach onto a neighbouring carriageway.

Flyovers and bridges

  • The car no longer jumps up onto a flyover when it drives underneath one, on HITEC City Road or anywhere else.
  • Only real flyovers and bridges are raised. On Banjara Hills a stretch of road that climbed up to a dead end is back on the ground.
  • Parapet walls no longer stand across the mouth of a ramp where it joins or leaves the main road.
  • The cable-stayed bridge's cables stay within the deck.

Scenery in the right place

  • Overlapping buildings, trees inside buildings and parked vehicles inside walls have been cleared.
  • Street stalls and carts stay on the kerb, never on a carriageway or a median.
  • Famous buildings, like City Center Mall, step back off the road, and the plaza round the Charminar is open again.
  • Lake shores on Tank Bund and Necklace Road follow the shore smoothly, and the water has less glare.
  • Shop awnings sit against their buildings.

Your theme, everywhere

If you chose light or dark on the website, the start page now opens the same way. Without a choice it follows your device's setting. There is no flash of the wrong theme while the page loads.

A toggle on the start page

The sun and moon button next to "Back to the website" switches the theme. Your choice is remembered and shared with the website, so both stay the same.

What stays dark

The drive itself, with its dashboard, guidance and pause screens, keeps its dark panels so they stay clear over the 3D city at any time of day. The loading screen and the "use a desktop or laptop" notice on phones follow your theme.
