Version 1.7.0 makes driving on a phone or tablet feel more like driving a car. The steering slider is now a steering wheel you turn with your thumb, and you can choose which side it goes.

A steering wheel you turn

  • Put your thumb on the wheel and turn it, as you would a real one. It follows your finger round.
  • Full lock is 150 degrees each way; half a turn of that gives half the steering, so small corrections stay small.
  • Let go and the wheel spins back to the centre as the car straightens.
  • The horn is in the middle of the wheel, as in a real car: press and hold the centre to sound it. (Added in 1.7.1.)

Indicators beside the wheel

The left and right indicators now sit on either side of the wheel, where your thumb already is. They light up while they are on and cancel themselves after the turn.

Wheel on the left or the right

On a phone or tablet, the start page options now include Steering wheel on: Left (pedals on the right) or Right (pedals on the left). Only the wheel and the pedals swap; the buttons at the top, the speed cluster and the directions stay where they are. Your choice is remembered.

Pedals set in from the edge

GO and BRAKE sit a little further in from the edges of the screen, so they are easier to reach without your thumb slipping off the side.

Sound that stops when you do

Added in 1.7.1, on every device:

  • When you reach the destination, the engine falls silent behind your debrief.
  • Pausing the drive pauses the sound too, and it comes back when you resume.
  • Switching to another tab or app, or turning the screen off, pauses the drive and silences it.
  • Closing the tab or the browser stops all sound straight away.

A fresh drive every time

Added in 1.7.2: every Start driving, Drive it again or Restart now begins a completely new session, built from fresh route data. A quick double tap starts one drive, not two, and coming back to the game with the browser's Back button loads it afresh instead of returning to an old, silent drive.
