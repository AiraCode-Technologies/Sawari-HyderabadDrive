This Hyderabad driving FAQ gives short, self-contained answers to the questions people ask most about driving in the city: getting a licence from the Telangana RTA, the traffic rules and e-challans, the main roads, peak hours, safety and how to practise first. Each answer links to a fuller guide where we have one.

Answers are correct to the best of our knowledge as of November 2026. Rules, fees and fines change, so for anything official check the Telangana Transport Department, Parivahan Sarathi or Hyderabad Traffic Police. We have left out fee and fine amounts on purpose.

Licence and RTA

How do I get a driving licence in Hyderabad?

Apply online for a learner's licence and pass its test, practise for at least 30 days, then book and pass a driving test at a Telangana RTA office. Our guide to getting a driving licence in Hyderabad covers each step.

Where do I apply?

On the national Parivahan Sarathi portal, choosing Telangana as your state. Fees are shown on the portal when you apply.

How long is a learner's licence valid?

Six months from issue. You must hold it for at least 30 days before you can take the driving test, so book the test inside that window.

What is the minimum age to drive a car?

18 years for a light motor vehicle (LMV) such as a car. The same age applies to geared motorcycles.

Can I drive on a learner's licence alone?

No. A learner must have a person with a valid licence for that class beside them, and the car must show L plates.

What happens in the RTA driving test?

An examiner checks that you control the car safely, follow the rules, use indicators and mirrors and complete the manoeuvres set at that office. Formats vary by office; our RTA driving test tips explain how to prepare.

Does an LMV licence cover automatic cars?

Yes. India has one LMV class with no separate automatic or manual category, so an LMV licence lets you drive both. See our guide on how to drive an automatic car in India.

Can I take the test in an automatic car?

The central government has said transmission need not be considered for an LMV licence, but practice at individual offices has varied. Check with your RTA office before you book.

Does my two-wheeler licence let me drive a car?

No. You need the LMV class added, which means a learner's licence for that class and a driving test in a car. Our two-wheeler to car tips help with the switch.

Can visitors and NRIs drive in Hyderabad on a foreign licence?

Generally foreign visitors carry an International Driving Permit along with their home licence, and long-term residents need an Indian licence. The details depend on your situation; see our guide to driving in Hyderabad for NRIs and visitors.

Which documents must I carry when driving?

Your driving licence, the vehicle's registration certificate, valid insurance and a pollution under control certificate. Digital copies in the official DigiLocker or mParivahan apps are accepted.

Rules and challans

What do terms like LMV, PUC and free left mean?

LMV is the light motor vehicle licence class for cars, PUC is the pollution under control certificate, and a free left is a left turn allowed on red where signed. Our Hyderabad driving glossary explains many more.

Which side of the road do you drive on in Hyderabad?

The left, as everywhere in India. Cars are right-hand drive, so you sit on the right with the centre line near your right shoulder.

How do I check for e-challans?

Search by your vehicle number on the official Hyderabad Traffic Police e-challan site. Pay or dispute only through official channels and ignore payment links sent by text or messaging apps.

How are most traffic offences caught?

Mostly on camera. Signal cameras, roadside cameras and officers with handheld cameras photograph the number plate, and an e-challan is raised against the registered owner.

Can I turn left on a red signal?

Only where a free left is signed or built as a separate slip lane. Everywhere else, red means stop for every direction. Our Hyderabad traffic rules guide explains free lefts.

Are seat belts compulsory in the back seat?

Seat belts are meant for everyone in the car, front and back. Make sure every passenger wears one on every trip.

Must the pillion rider wear a helmet?

Yes. Both the rider and the pillion on a two-wheeler must wear a fastened protective helmet, and Hyderabad Traffic Police enforce it for both.

What is the drink-driving limit?

30 mg of alcohol per 100 ml of blood, which very little alcohol can exceed. Police run regular breath tests, especially at night and weekends, so do not drink if you are driving.

Can I use my phone while driving?

No, not held in your hand, including at a red light. Mount it for navigation, set the route before you move and pull over safely to make a call.

What is the speed limit in Hyderabad?

There is no single city-wide figure. Limits depend on the road and are posted on signs, and they are lower near schools, hospitals and markets; see our guide to speed breakers and speed limits.

What should I do when an ambulance comes up behind me?

Move left as far as you safely can and slow down or stop to let it through. Since the 2019 Motor Vehicles Act amendments, failing to give way to an emergency vehicle is a specific offence.

Do I need to change my TS number plate to TG?

No. TG applies to vehicles registered from March 2024, and existing TS registrations stay valid. Our guide to TG number plates has the details.

Roads and routes

Why are there so many U-turns instead of right turns?

On many busy roads the traffic police have closed right turns at junctions so signals can give more time to through traffic. You continue and turn back at a median opening; see U-turns in Hyderabad.

What is the Outer Ring Road?

The Nehru Outer Ring Road is an eight-lane, access-controlled expressway of about 158 km around the city, with distance-based FASTag tolls. Our ORR guide covers exits and safety.

What is the speed limit on the ORR?

Since 2023, 120 km/h in the two lanes nearest the median and 80 km/h in the two outer lanes. Follow the signs, which can differ near interchanges.

Can two-wheelers and autos use the ORR?

No. Two-wheelers and three-wheelers are not allowed on the main carriageway; they can use the service roads alongside much of it.

What is the best way to drive to the airport?

From the centre and west, most drivers use the PVNR Expressway from Mehdipatnam; from the IT corridor and outer suburbs, the ORR is usually simpler. See driving to Hyderabad airport.

Is it hard to drive near the Charminar?

Yes, it is the hardest part of the city: narrow one-way streets, heavy crowds and very little parking. Go early, or park further away and walk; see our Old City driving guide.

Which roads are good for a first drive?

Wide, steady roads with few turns, such as Tank Bund Road and Necklace Road around Hussain Sagar, early in the morning. Leave busy commercial areas and the Old City until later.

Where can I park in Hyderabad?

Use marked bays, pay parking and the car parks at malls, offices and some metro stations, never footpaths, junctions or bus stops. Our Hyderabad parking guide goes into detail.

Can I hire a self-drive car in Hyderabad?

Yes, several self-drive rental services operate in the city, and they generally ask for a valid licence and identity documents. See our guide to self-drive car rental in Hyderabad.

Traffic and timing

What are the peak traffic hours?

As of November 2026, traffic police advisories ask commuters to avoid 8.30 to 11 am and 5 to 9 pm on affected roads. Our guide to the best time to drive in Hyderabad breaks it down by area.

When is the best time to drive?

Early morning, before about 8 am, and weekend mornings are the calmest of all. Mid-afternoon on weekdays is often lighter than the peaks.

Which areas have the worst traffic?

The IT corridor around HITEC City, Madhapur and Gachibowli is the most often reported, along with Kukatpally, Ameerpet, Panjagutta, Mehdipatnam, LB Nagar and Secunderabad. See traffic hotspots and peak hours.

Is driving in Hyderabad hard?

It is demanding at first because of mixed traffic, two-wheelers and peak-hour jams, but wide roads and repeating patterns make it learnable. Read our honest answer and step-by-step plan.

Are roads closed on New Year's Eve?

Usually some are. On 31 December 2025 police closed most flyovers except Begumpet and Tolichowki, and closed Tank Bund and Necklace Road to vehicles from 11 pm to 2 am; check each year's advisory.

Which festivals cause road closures?

Ganesh immersion closes or restricts Tank Bund, Necklace Road and NTR Marg, Bonalu brings diversions around major temples in July and August, and Ramzan nights crowd the Old City. Police publish advisories beforehand.

Where can I find traffic advisories?

On the websites and social media of Hyderabad Traffic Police, Cyberabad Police and Rachakonda Police. The Hyderabad traffic helpline quoted in 2026 advisories is 9010203626.

Safety

How should I deal with two-wheelers?

Expect them on both sides, check mirrors often, indicate early and change direction gradually. Our guide to sharing the road covers autos and buses too.

Is it safe to drive through a flooded road?

Only if you can see it is shallow, ideally by watching a similar car cross first. If you cannot judge the depth or the water is moving, turn back; see our monsoon driving guide.

Is night driving in Hyderabad safe?

It is fine for steady drivers, with less traffic but more glare, some faster drivers and unlit hazards. Leave it until your daytime driving is confident; see our night driving tips.

How should I take speed breakers?

Slowly enough that the car rolls over without a jolt, roughly walking to jogging pace. Expect unmarked humps on colony roads, near schools and at temple gates.

What should I do after a minor accident?

Stop safely, switch on the hazard lights, check for injuries and call 112 for emergency help if anyone is hurt. Take photos, exchange details and inform your insurer; stay calm and avoid arguments at the roadside.

Practising with Sawari

What is Sawari?

Sawari is a free browser 3D driving simulator on real Hyderabad roads, built from OpenStreetMap. Traffic keeps left, the car is a right-hand-drive automatic and the drive ends with a debrief listing every fault.

Which routes can I drive?

Five: Tank Bund Road, Necklace Road, Charminar and Pathergatti, Cyber Towers to Jubilee Hills over the Durgam Cheruvu cable bridge, and Banjara Hills Road No. 1. See all routes.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Hold the phone sideways and steer with an on-screen steering wheel; it also runs on tablets, laptops and PCs, with nothing to install and no sign-up.

Can I challenge a friend?

Yes. At the end of a drive you can send a link that challenges a friend to beat your score on the same route, time of day and traffic.

Can Sawari replace driving lessons?

No. It helps with layouts, rules and habits such as indicating and stopping at the line, but it cannot teach real car control. Use it alongside lessons with a licensed instructor; our guide to driving simulators in India explains what practice on screen can and cannot do.
