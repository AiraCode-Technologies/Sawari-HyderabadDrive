Version 1.1.0 fixes the metro and road problems on the HITEC City route, gives every route new street lights, makes your car's indicators easier to see and starts this blog.

Metro pillars and stations

  • No metro pillar stands in a traffic lane any more; each one moves to the median or the footpath, or is left out.
  • Station columns stand clear of every lane, and the station deck sits high enough for buses and lorries to pass under.
  • Station name boards sit on the concourse, not hanging over the road.

Roads on hills

  • The two halves of a divided road are level with each other, instead of one sitting a metre or two below the other on a hillside.
  • Side roads ease out of a junction rather than dropping straight off the main road.
  • The paved strip beside a road stops where a side road joins, so zebra crossings stay white stripes on the asphalt.

People and the road

Pedestrians no longer walk along another road's carriageway or under a low flyover deck. They still cross side-road mouths, and wait for traffic when they do.

Your car and the street

  • The indicators on your car are about twice the size and brighter when they blink.
  • New street lights on every route: a concrete plinth, a tapered pole, a swan-neck arm and an LED lantern, with double arms on medians.

The Sawari blog

This blog launched with this version: route guides, learning to drive, road skills and how the city is built. Start with the HITEC City to Jubilee Hills drive, then try it in the simulator.
