Version 1.0.0 is the first numbered release of Sawari. It makes the traffic around you behave like real drivers, steadies the driver's view, adds traffic signals at busy junctions and rebuilds the surroundings of Necklace Road.

Traffic that drives like people do

  • Cars, buses, autos and two-wheelers now look ahead along their lane and through the next junction, and keep a gap that grows with their speed.
  • Where two paths cross, the drivers settle who goes first, and the other waits instead of pushing through.
  • Two vehicles that block each other sort it out: one reverses to make room, then follows.
  • Crashes between other drivers are rare and random; the one at fault backs off and the other goes first.
  • If you stop in a lane, traffic queues, honks and then drives round you when the way is clear.
  • Pedestrians keep left, step aside for each other and wait for vehicles before crossing a side road.

A steady driver's view

The camera in the driver's seat used the car's position from the frame before, so the dashboard shook against the road at speed. It now moves with the car exactly. Distant road markings no longer flicker in the driver's view either.

More traffic signals

Busy junctions along every route that had no mapped signals now have them, and every signal at one junction runs on the same cycle. Signal poles stand on the kerb, never on the road. See our guide to Hyderabad's traffic rules for how to approach them.

Necklace Road, as it really is

  • The lakefront stays open: no invented buildings between the road and Hussain Sagar, or squeezed against the railway.
  • The MMTS stations (Necklace Road, Sanjeevaiah Park, James Street and Khairatabad) now have platforms, shelters and name boards.
  • Trees line the railway, and parked vehicles keep off every carriageway.

On the website

The footer now shows the version number, so you can always tell which release you are playing. Ready to try it? Drive Necklace Road.
