In Telangana you renew a driving licence on the national Parivahan Sarathi portal, and since May 2026 most renewals can be done online with Aadhaar authentication, without visiting the RTA office. Apply any time from one year before your licence expires; there is no grace period, so do not drive on an expired licence.

This guide explains how long a licence is valid, when and how to renew, and how to get a duplicate licence, change your address or apply for an international driving permit, as of November 2026. We have left out fees, because they change; the Sarathi portal shows the exact amount before you pay. If you do not have a licence yet, start with our guide to getting a driving licence in Hyderabad.

How long is a driving licence valid?

A private licence for cars and two-wheelers is valid until a set age or for a set number of years, depending on how old you are when it is issued or renewed. The rule is in Section 14 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, as amended.

Your age when the licence is issued or renewedHow long it is valid
Under 30Until you turn 40
30 to under 50Ten years
50 to under 55Until you turn 60
55 or olderFive years
Transport vehicle licence (any age)Five years (three years for vehicles carrying hazardous goods)

A learner's licence is valid for six months. The expiry date is printed on your licence card and shown in the DigiLocker and mParivahan apps, which is the quickest way to check. In mid-2026 there were reports that the Centre was looking at longer validity, but as of November 2026 the rule above still applies.

When should you renew, and is there a grace period?

Renew in the year before your licence expires, because there is no longer any grace period after expiry. Since the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act, 2019 came into force:

  • You can apply up to one year before expiry.
  • The old 30-day grace period is gone. Once the date passes, you are driving without a valid licence, which is an offence and can cause problems with an insurance claim.
  • You can still renew within one year after expiry, although a late fee may apply.
  • After more than a year, the law allows the authority to require you to pass the test of competence again.
Put the expiry date in your phone calendar with a reminder eleven months ahead. It is the simplest way to never drive on an expired licence.

How do you renew a driving licence online in Telangana?

Use the Driving Licence services on the Sarathi portal, choose Telangana, and pick renewal. Telangana completed its move to Sarathi across all RTA offices in 2025, and in May 2026 the Transport Department made 17 licence services available in faceless mode with Aadhaar-based authentication, including renewal, duplicate licences, replacement and changes to photo, signature and date of birth.

  1. Open sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, go to the driving licence services and select Telangana.
  2. Choose the renewal service and enter your licence number and date of birth.
  3. Complete Aadhaar authentication with the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
  4. Check the details, upload what the form asks for and pay the fee online.
  5. Keep the application number. The renewed licence is updated in DigiLocker and mParivahan, and the card is usually posted to the address on record.

If the portal asks you to visit the RTA, for example for biometrics or a test, it lets you book a slot. The Telangana Transport Department site lists the RTA offices and current notices.

Documents, in general terms

  • Your current or expired driving licence.
  • Aadhaar for authentication, with your current mobile number linked.
  • A recent photo and signature, if the form asks you to upload them.
  • A medical certificate in Form 1A from a registered doctor if you are 40 or older, or if you hold a transport licence.
Apply only on the official Sarathi portal or at the RTA office. Agents and look-alike websites that charge extra for "fast renewal" are common, and they may misuse your Aadhaar details.

How do you get a duplicate licence or change the address?

Both are online services on Sarathi, under the same Telangana driving licence services.

Duplicate licence

If your licence card is lost, stolen or damaged, apply for a duplicate on Sarathi. It is one of the faceless services in Telangana. For a lost or stolen card, keep a copy of a police complaint or the declaration the form asks for. Until the duplicate arrives, the digital licence in DigiLocker or mParivahan is accepted at checks, because the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways treats documents in those two apps as equal to the originals.

Change of address

If you have moved, apply for a change of address on the licence through Sarathi with proof of your new address. Your vehicle's registration certificate is a separate record, so update the address on the RC too, through the vehicle services on Parivahan. If you have moved to Hyderabad from another state, the RTA has asked people to update their licence details through the Parivahan portal; our guide for NRIs and visitors driving in Hyderabad covers licences from elsewhere.

How do you get an international driving permit in Hyderabad?

Apply to the Telangana RTA through Sarathi using Form 4A, if you hold a valid Indian licence and are travelling abroad. Key points:

  • Who it is for: residents with a valid Indian licence who want to drive in another country for a short stay. It translates your Indian licence; it is not a licence on its own, so carry both.
  • Validity: generally one year from issue, and not beyond the expiry of your Indian licence. Renew your Indian licence first if it is close to expiry.
  • What you usually need: your licence, passport, visa or travel details where asked, photos and, in some cases, a medical certificate.
  • Visit: unlike renewal, the permit is not in Telangana's faceless list, so expect to visit the RTA office for verification.
  • Check the destination: some countries do not recognise the permit, or require a local licence after a period of residence. Check with that country's embassy.

Key facts at a glance

  • Portal: Parivahan Sarathi, Telangana selected.
  • Renewal window: one year before expiry to one year after; no grace period for driving.
  • Faceless services: 17 licence services online with Aadhaar since May 2026, including renewal and duplicates.
  • Over 40: a Form 1A medical certificate is generally needed.
  • Digital copies: licences in DigiLocker or mParivahan are valid at checks; a photo in your gallery is not.
  • IDP: Form 4A, generally valid for one year, visit the RTA.
