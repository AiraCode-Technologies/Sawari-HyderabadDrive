Version 1.9.0 turns every drive into something you can share. At the end of a route you can post a score card or send a friend a challenge to beat you on the same road.

Your score card

  • Tap Share score card on the results screen to make a picture sized for WhatsApp status and Instagram Stories.
  • It shows the moment you arrived, straight from the game, with the route, your score and stars, your time, distance and faults.
  • Add your name if you like, and the card says "Your name drove it. Can you beat it?" with the link to Sawari.
  • On a phone it opens the share sheet, so you can send it to WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere else. On a computer the picture downloads and the message is copied for you to paste.

Challenge a friend

  • Tap Challenge a friend to send a link with your score in it.
  • Your friend's start page says who challenged them and with what score, with the same route, time of day and traffic already picked.
  • When they finish, the results screen tells them who won, and they can send a challenge straight back.
  • There is no account or sign-up: the challenge travels inside the link.

Try it

Drive Tank Bund Road, then challenge a friend to beat your score.
