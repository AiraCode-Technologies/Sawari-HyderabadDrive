Version 1.12.0 makes it easy to tell us what you think. Found a bug, have an idea, or want a road of your own added? Send feedback straight from the game.

Where to find it

  • On the start page, below the options.
  • On the pause screen, if something goes wrong mid-drive.
  • On the results screen, after every drive.
  • In the website footer, under "Send feedback", in English, Hindi, Urdu and Telugu.

What to send

Choose whether it is something broken, an idea, a route you want or something else, and write a few words. Your name and email are optional; leave an email if you would like a reply. The form also tells us which route you were on, the Sawari version, your kind of device and screen size, so we can reproduce problems. Nothing else is collected.

Also fixed

The "See the routes" button at the end of every blog article now opens the routes page.

Try it

Send us feedback, or take a drive first.
