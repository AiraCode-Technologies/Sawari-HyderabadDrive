Built to feel like a drive across Hyderabad

Realistic car handling, local traffic, a proper dashboard and the city from morning to night. All of it runs in a browser tab.

Driving

A car that handles like a car

The physics model has tyre grip and slip, weight transfer under braking and cornering, a six-speed automatic and ABS. Off the road the grip changes, and the suspension bounces over kerbs and speed breakers.

  • Right-hand drive cockpit with working instruments
  • Centre screen with clock, radio and trip details (no map)
  • Chase view or driver's seat, with drag-to-look
Streets and city life

Busy, crowded, unmistakably local

Traffic

23 kinds of vehicle: hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, scooters, autos, buses and lorries. Bikes filter through gaps, buses stop and traffic obeys the signals.

Buildings by area

Arched Old City facades, HITEC City glass towers and Banjara Hills villas, plus temples, mosques and churches from the map.

Telugu and English

Real shop names where the map has them, local-style names elsewhere, and roadside stalls, hoardings and parked two-wheelers.

Proper roads

Textured asphalt with worn lane lines, raised kerbs, tiled footpaths, medians with U-turn openings, flyovers and metro viaducts.

Guidance

A blue band for your lane, turn-by-turn directions with an optional voice, and a steering guide that warns you before you leave the road.

Road rules

Red lights, speeding, missed indicators, collisions and speed breakers are logged and explained in your debrief.

Dashboard and controls

Everything at your fingertips

The on-screen cluster shows speed and revs, your gear, the speed limit, indicators and warning lamps. It also works with a gamepad.

Time of day and graphics

Morning traffic or a night drive

Sun, sky and shadows change with the time you choose. After dark, street lamps, windows and every vehicle's lights come on.

MorningLow, warm sun and long shadows
MiddayBright, clear and high sun
EveningOrange sky and lamps coming on
NightStreet lamps and lit windows

Runs on ordinary laptops

The city is drawn in tiles, so only what you can see is rendered. If a computer can't keep up, the resolution adjusts itself.

Two graphics settings

High adds sharp sun shadows; Fast is for older machines. Both keep the full city and traffic.

See it for yourself

Try the evening light on Tank Bund, or a night drive through the Old City.
