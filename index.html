Why it feels like Hyderabad

Not a generic city: the streets you know

Every route is built from OpenStreetMap and real elevation data, then filled with the life you see on these roads every day.

Real roads and terrain

Real lane counts, medians, flyovers and gradients. Banjara Hills Road No. 1 really does climb 37 metres.

Traffic that behaves locally

Autos, buses, scooters that filter past, people on the footpath and crowds outside temples and mosques.

A car that drives properly

Tyre grip, weight transfer, an automatic gearbox and ABS, from a chase view or the driver's seat.

How it works

From the menu to your score in minutes

Pick a route and a time

Choose a drive, the traffic level and morning, midday, evening or night.

Follow the blue band

Keep left, signal your turns and listen for the spoken directions. Signals, speed limits and speed breakers all count.

Read your debrief

Get a score, every fault with advice on avoiding it, and a personal best for each route.

Day and night

The city after dark

At night, street lamps light the road, windows light up across the city and every vehicle shows its head and tail lamps. Switch on your headlights with L and take a slower lap.

  • Morning, midday, evening and night lighting
  • Telugu and English shop signs and hoardings
  • Temples, mosques and churches along the way
