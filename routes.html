Difficulty

Which route should you start with?

The level reflects the number of turns, how busy the junctions are and how tight the streets get.

Easy

Getting comfortable

No turns to make: settle into a lane and get a feel for the car and the traffic.

Routes: Tank Bund Road, Necklace Road.

Medium

Junctions and flyovers

A handful of turns, flyovers and busy junctions.

Routes: Cyber Towers to Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills Road No. 1.

Hard

Old City streets

Tight Old City streets, a roundabout and nine turns in two kilometres.

Routes: Charminar and Pathergatti.

Practice tips

Drive it the way the debrief marks it

  • Keep left: the blue band shows your lane on the route.
  • Indicate before every turn with Q or E. The indicator cancels itself after the turn.
  • Stop at the line on red; amber is not a reason to speed up.
  • Slow down for speed breakers. You will feel them if you don't.
  • Missed a turn? Look for the blue U-turn sign at the next median opening.
