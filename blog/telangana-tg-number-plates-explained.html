Telangana TG number plates explained: codes, HSRP, BH series and plate colours

TG number plates in Telangana explained: the TS to TG change, Hyderabad RTA codes TG 07 to TG 13, HSRP rules, the BH series and what each plate colour means.

A TG number plate tells you a vehicle was registered in Telangana, and the two digits after it tell you which transport office registered it. This guide explains the change from TS to TG, what the Hyderabad codes mean, how the high-security plate (HSRP) rules apply in Telangana, how the BH series works and what each plate colour signals.

How an Indian number plate is read

A standard Indian registration mark has four parts. Take TG 09 AB 1234 as an example:

  • TG: the state or union territory code, here Telangana.
  • 09: the code of the regional transport office (in Telangana, the RTA office or district) where the vehicle was registered.
  • AB: a letter series, issued in sequence as numbers run out. Fancy numbers within a series are auctioned.
  • 1234: the number itself, from 0001 to 9999.

The office code shows where the vehicle was first registered, not where the owner lives now. A car registered in Khairatabad keeps its TG 09 number if the owner moves to Gachibowli.

From TS to TG number plates: what changed in 2024

When Telangana was formed in June 2014, its vehicles were given the code TS. In March 2024 the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways issued a notification, dated 12 March 2024, changing Telangana's code to TG, and the Telangana Transport Department began registering new vehicles with TG from 15 March 2024.

Two points cause most of the confusion:

  • Existing TS plates stay valid. The change applies only to vehicles registered after it came in. A car registered as TS 09 in 2022 remains TS 09; there is nothing to apply for.
  • Do not change TS to TG yourself. Transport officials have warned owners against this. TS 09 AB 1234 and TG 09 AB 1234 can be two different vehicles, so altering your plate could give your car another vehicle's number. It is also not a legal registration mark for your vehicle.

Before 2014, Hyderabad vehicles were registered under Andhra Pradesh with the code AP, and you will still see a few on older vehicles.

Telangana RTA codes for Hyderabad and around

Within Hyderabad, the transport department divides the city into zones, each with its own code. Around the city, the neighbouring districts have theirs. These are the codes you will see most often on Hyderabad roads:

CodeArea
TG 07Ranga Reddy district
TG 08Medchal-Malkajgiri district
TG 09Hyderabad Central zone (Khairatabad)
TG 10Hyderabad North zone
TG 11Hyderabad East zone
TG 12Hyderabad South zone
TG 13Hyderabad West zone

The same numbers were used with TS before March 2024, so TS 09 and TG 09 both mean the Central zone. Ranga Reddy (TG 07) and Medchal-Malkajgiri (TG 08) cover much of the growing west and north of the city, including large parts of the IT corridor and Kukatpally, so you will see plenty of both in HITEC City traffic.

Offices and the areas they serve can be reorganised, and each district has several offices. To find which office covers your address, check the Telangana Transport Department website, which lists the state and district codes.

Registering a new vehicle is usually handled by the dealer, but the office that serves your address decides the code. If you are going for your own licence too, our guide to getting a driving licence in Hyderabad covers the RTA process.

High-security registration plates (HSRP) in Telangana

A high-security registration plate is an aluminium plate with a chromium hologram, a blue "IND" strip, a laser-etched serial number, and non-removable snap locks so it cannot be easily swapped. A third registration mark, a sticker on the windscreen, matches the plates.

New vehicles

Under central rules, new vehicles sold in India since April 2019 come with HSRP fitted by the manufacturer's dealer. If you bought your car new after that, you should already have them.

Older vehicles

In April 2025 the Telangana government ordered that vehicles registered before 1 April 2019 must also be fitted with HSRP, with a deadline of 30 September 2025, and said action would be taken under the Motor Vehicles Act for non-compliance. It applies to all classes of vehicle, including two-wheelers. At the time of writing (November 2026), check the Transport Department for the current position on enforcement and any later changes.

Fake websites offering HSRP booking have been reported in Telangana. Book only through the official portal linked from the Transport Department website, and never pay through a link sent by message.

BH series: the Bharat registration

The BH (Bharat) series was introduced by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways through a notification dated 26 August 2021, in force from 15 September 2021. It is meant for people who move between states for work, so they do not have to re-register their vehicle each time.

A BH plate reads like 24 BH 1234 AA: the year of first registration, BH, a four-digit number and a letter series. There is no state or office code.

  • Who can apply: it is voluntary and open to defence personnel, employees of central and state governments and their public sector undertakings, and employees of private companies with offices in four or more states or union territories.
  • Vehicles: private, non-transport vehicles only.
  • Road tax: paid for two years at a time (or multiples of two) instead of in one lump sum. The notified rates are 8 per cent for vehicles up to Rs 10 lakh, 10 per cent between Rs 10 lakh and Rs 20 lakh and 12 per cent above that, with 2 per cent less for electric vehicles. After the fourteenth year the tax becomes annual, at half the earlier rate.

Read the official Press Information Bureau release on the BH series for the full terms.

The green plates for electric vehicles come from a 2018 notification of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways. Hybrids are not battery electric vehicles and keep ordinary white or yellow plates. If you are thinking of going electric, our guide to electric cars in Hyderabad covers road tax and charging.

Plate colour matters to drivers too. A yellow plate warns you a vehicle may stop suddenly to pick up or drop passengers, as autos and cabs do all over the city; our post on sharing the road with autos, buses and two-wheelers has more.

Number plates in Sawari

The car you drive in Sawari carries a Telangana-style white plate, TS 09 EH 4521, with the blue IND strip of a high-security plate. In plate terms that is a private car registered at the Hyderabad Central zone before the 2024 change to TG. The number is invented, so it does not belong to any real vehicle. The traffic around you is generic, so look at the road, not the plates. You can start a drive free on a phone, tablet, laptop or desktop.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to change my TS number plate to TG?

No. The TG code applies to vehicles registered from March 2024 onwards. Existing TS registrations remain valid, and owners must not change TS to TG on their own plates, because the result could clash with a different vehicle's genuine TG number.

What does TG 09 mean on a number plate?

TG 09 is the code for the Hyderabad Central zone, the regional transport office at Khairatabad. The other Hyderabad zones are TG 10 (North), TG 11 (East), TG 12 (South) and TG 13 (West).

Is HSRP mandatory in Telangana?

New vehicles across India have been fitted with high-security registration plates since April 2019. In April 2025 Telangana ordered HSRP for older vehicles registered before 1 April 2019 too. Book only through the official portal linked from the Transport Department website.

Who can get a BH series number plate?

The Bharat series is voluntary and open to defence personnel, central and state government and public sector employees, and employees of private companies with offices in four or more states or union territories. It is for private, non-transport vehicles.
