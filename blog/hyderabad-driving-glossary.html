Hyderabad driving glossary: 60 road, traffic and licence terms explained

A Hyderabad driving glossary: RTA, LLR, e-challan, HSRP, TG plates, FASTag, ORR, RRR, PVNR Expressway, SRDP, free left, share autos and more, in plain English.

This Hyderabad driving glossary explains about 60 terms you meet on the city's roads, at the RTA and in traffic advisories, from "free left" and "U-turn opening" to ORR, SRDP, HSRP and e-challan. Each definition is short and self-contained, and is correct as we understand it in November 2026.

First, a quick map of who runs what.

A to D

Auto (auto-rickshaw): A three-wheeled taxi with a small engine, seating up to three passengers.

BH series: The Bharat series registration, a plate in the format "YY BH 1234 AA" for people who move between states for work, so they need not re-register the vehicle in each state.

Bus bay: A recess at the kerb where buses are meant to stop. Expect buses to pull out of it.

Cable bridge: In Hyderabad, usually the Durgam Cheruvu cable-stayed bridge, which carries traffic over Durgam Cheruvu lake between Jubilee Hills Road No. 45 and the Madhapur and HITEC City side. It opened in September 2020.

Challan: An official notice of a traffic offence with a fine to pay.

Check post: A name kept by some junctions that were once checkpoints, such as Jubilee Hills Check Post.

Commissionerate: A police unit covering a city area, each with its own traffic police. Since December 2025 the Hyderabad region has four: Hyderabad, Cyberabad, Malkajgiri and Future City.

Cyberabad: The popular name for the western IT district around HITEC City, Madhapur and Gachibowli, and the name of the police commissionerate that covers it. Since 2026 it is also the name of a municipal corporation.

Diversion: A temporary change of route ordered by traffic police for road works, events, rallies or flooding.

DL: Driving licence. In Telangana it is issued by the RTA after a learner's licence and a driving test, and a digital copy in DigiLocker or mParivahan is accepted.

E to H

e-challan: A traffic fine issued electronically, often from a camera photo, and linked to the vehicle's registration number. You can check and pay pending e-challans on the Telangana e-challan portal.

Elevated corridor: A long flyover that carries traffic over several junctions in one go, such as the Aramghar to Zoo Park flyover.

FASTag: A windscreen sticker with a radio tag that pays tolls from a linked account. It has been mandatory for four-wheelers on national highways since 2021 and is the normal way to pay on the ORR.

Financial District: The office district around Nanakramguda and Gachibowli, west of HITEC City.

Flyover: A bridge that lifts traffic over a junction or a stretch of road. See our guide to Hyderabad flyovers and underpasses.

Free left: A left turn you may make on red where a sign, a green left arrow or a slip lane allows it. Give way as you turn, and never block the free left lane if you are going straight.

Future City: A planned new urban area on the southern side of the Hyderabad region, beyond the ORR. It has had its own police commissionerate since December 2025.

GHMC: Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation, the civic body for city roads and parking. It was greatly enlarged in December 2025, and in February 2026 the Cyberabad and Malkajgiri corporations were split off from it.

HITEC City: Hyderabad Information Technology and Engineering Consultancy City, the IT district in Madhapur built around the Cyber Towers building.

HMDA: Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority, which plans the wider metropolitan region and built the Outer Ring Road.

HMRL: Hyderabad Metro Rail Limited, the state company behind the metro. The Telangana government took full ownership of the Phase I network from L&T in 2026.

HSRP: High-security registration plate, a tamper-proof aluminium plate with a hologram and laser-etched code. New vehicles have had them since April 2019, and Telangana ordered them for older vehicles in 2025.

HTP: Hyderabad Traffic Police, the traffic wing of the Hyderabad commissionerate, which posts diversions on its website.

I to O

IDP: International Driving Permit, a translation of a licence in a standard format. Foreign visitors use one issued at home with their home licence; see our guide for NRIs and visitors.

Interchange: A grade-separated junction where you join or leave an expressway by ramps, as at each exit of the ORR.

IT corridor: The western belt of office districts, including HITEC City, Madhapur, Kondapur, Gachibowli and the Financial District.

LLR: Learner's licence, the first step to a driving licence. It is valid for six months and lets you drive only with a licensed driver beside you and L plates displayed.

LMV: Light motor vehicle, the licence class for ordinary cars and jeeps.

Malkajgiri: An area in the north-east of the city that gave its name to a police commissionerate (formerly Rachakonda) in December 2025 and to a municipal corporation in 2026.

Median: The raised divider down the middle of a road, separating the two directions. Breaks in it are used for U-turns.

Metro pillar number: The number painted on each pillar of the elevated metro, used as a landmark for addresses and cab pick-ups.

MMTS: Multi-Modal Transport System, the suburban train service run by South Central Railway on the city's railway lines.

Necklace Road: The lakeside road along the western and northern shores of Hussain Sagar.

NH 44: The national highway that runs through Hyderabad from north to south, carrying traffic towards Nagpur in one direction and Bengaluru and the airport side in the other.

Old City: The historic area south of the Musi river around the Charminar, with narrow, crowded streets and markets.

ORR: The Outer Ring Road, officially the Nehru Outer Ring Road, a 158 km, eight-lane access-controlled expressway around the city with tolls by distance.

P to R

Pathergatti: The stone-arcaded market street leading north from the Charminar towards the Musi.

PUC: Pollution Under Control certificate, proof that a vehicle's emissions passed a test.

PVNR Expressway: The PV Narasimha Rao Expressway, an elevated road of about 11.6 km from Mehdipatnam to Aramghar, on the main route to the airport.

RC: Registration certificate, the document that proves a vehicle is registered and shows its owner.

ROB and RUB: Road-over-bridge and road-under-bridge, which take a road over or under a railway line instead of across a level crossing.

Rachakonda: The former name of the police commissionerate covering the eastern side of the city, renamed Malkajgiri in December 2025. The old name still appears on some signs.

RRR: Regional Ring Road, a planned second ring road of about 340 km far outside the ORR. As of November 2026 it was not yet built. See our RRR guide.

RTA: Regional Transport Authority, the local office of the Telangana Transport Department for licences, registration and driving tests. Often called the RTO.

Rumble strips: Rows of low ridges across the road that make the car vibrate to warn you of a hazard ahead.

S to T

Service road: A slower road alongside a main road or expressway, giving access to buildings.

Share auto: A larger auto or small van that runs on a fixed route and picks up several passengers at once.

Slip road: A short road that lets traffic leave or join a main road, or turn left, without going through the junction.

Speed breaker: Also called a hump, a raised bump across the road to slow traffic, often unpainted.

SRDP: Strategic Road Development Plan, a programme to build flyovers, underpasses, road-over-bridges and steel bridges at the most congested junctions.

Steel bridge: A flyover built with a steel deck rather than concrete. The best known is the Indira Park to VST steel bridge of about 2.6 km, opened in August 2023 and named after Nayini Narsimha Reddy.

Tank Bund: The road along the dam on the eastern side of Hussain Sagar, built in the sixteenth century, linking Hyderabad and Secunderabad.

TG plate: A Telangana registration plate. The state code changed from TS to TG for new registrations from March 2024; older TS plates remain valid.

TGSRTC: Telangana State Road Transport Corporation, which runs the city's buses. Many people still call it TSRTC, its name before 2024.

Toll plaza: A row of booths where tolls are collected, on the ORR and national highways.

Towing: Removal of wrongly parked vehicles by traffic police. You pay a towing charge and a fine to recover it.

U to Z

U-turn opening: A break in the median where you turn back to go the other way. On many main roads it replaces the right turn at the junction. See U-turns in Hyderabad.

Underpass: A road that dips under a junction or another road so traffic can pass without stopping. Low ones can flood in heavy rain.

Wrong-side driving: Driving against the flow of traffic, often to avoid a long U-turn. It causes head-on crashes and is fined.

Zebra crossing: Black and white stripes marking a pedestrian crossing. Stop before it, not on it, at a red signal.

Frequently asked questions

What does RTA mean in Hyderabad?

RTA stands for Regional Transport Authority, the local office of the Telangana Transport Department that issues driving licences, registers vehicles and conducts driving tests. People often use RTA and RTO to mean the same thing.

What is a free left in Hyderabad?

A free left is a left turn you may make even when the main signal is red, where a sign, a green left arrow or a separate slip lane shows it is allowed. You must still give way to pedestrians and traffic, and you should never block a free left lane if you are going straight.

What is the difference between the ORR and the RRR?

The ORR is the 158 km Outer Ring Road expressway that already loops round Hyderabad. The RRR is the Regional Ring Road, a much larger second ring of about 340 km that is planned far outside the ORR and was not yet built as of November 2026.

What is SRDP in Hyderabad?

SRDP is the Strategic Road Development Plan, a programme to build flyovers, underpasses, road-over-bridges and steel bridges at Hyderabad's most congested junctions.
