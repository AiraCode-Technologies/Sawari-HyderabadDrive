Car maintenance checklist for India: weekly and monthly checks, monsoon prep and your PUC certificate

A car maintenance checklist for India: weekly and monthly checks on tyres, lights, wipers, fluids and brakes, monsoon prep, and PUC certificate validity rules.

A simple car maintenance routine is a quick weekly walk-round for tyres, lights and leaks, a monthly check of tyre pressures, fluids, wipers and brakes, and the servicing your owner's manual sets out. On top of that, every car in India must carry a valid Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate, and from November 2026 its validity depends on the car's emission standard and age.

You do not need to be a mechanic to look after a car. Most problems that leave people stranded on a Hyderabad flyover, such as a soft tyre, a dead bulb or an overheated engine, show warning signs that a few minutes of checking would catch. Always follow your owner's manual first; this is a general checklist.

What should you check every week?

Every week, walk round the car before you drive, look at the tyres, test the lights and glance underneath for leaks.

  • Tyres: look for one that seems lower than the others, cuts, bulges on the sidewall, or nails in the tread.
  • Lights: headlights (low and high beam), indicators front and back, hazard lights, brake lights and number-plate light. Ask someone to stand behind the car while you press the brake, or check the reflection in a glass door.
  • Glass and mirrors: clean, with no cracks spreading across the windscreen.
  • Leaks: fresh drips under the car where it was parked. Clear water under the front on a hot day is usually just the air conditioner.
  • Warning lights: when you start the car, the dashboard lamps should come on briefly and go out. Any that stay on need attention.

What should you check every month?

Once a month, check tyre pressures and tread, the main fluid levels, the wipers and how the brakes feel.

Electric cars have no engine oil or exhaust, but tyres, brakes, lights, wipers, coolant and washer fluid still need checking. See our guide to electric cars in Hyderabad.

Beyond these checks, get the car serviced at the intervals in the owner's manual, by distance or by time, whichever comes first. Hyderabad's stop-start traffic and summer heat are hard on brakes, clutches and cooling systems, so do not stretch service intervals.

How should you prepare a car for the monsoon?

Before the rains, make sure you can see, stop and grip: wipers, lights, tyres and brakes matter most.

  1. Fit new wiper blades if the old ones have been baked by the summer sun.
  2. Check tyre tread. Worn tyres cannot clear water and lose grip on wet roads.
  3. Test all lights, including fog lamps if fitted, and clean the lenses.
  4. Have the brakes inspected if they have not been looked at recently.
  5. Check the demister and the air conditioner, which clear a misted windscreen quickly.
  6. Clear the drains below the windscreen and in the sunroof, if you have one, so water does not leak into the cabin.
  7. Keep a torch, an umbrella and a phone charger in the car.

Do not drive through deep water at underpasses or low junctions. Water drawn into the engine can wreck it. Our monsoon driving guide covers driving in the rain itself.

What is a PUC certificate, and how long is it valid?

A PUC certificate shows that your vehicle's exhaust emissions were tested and found within the permitted limits. Every motor vehicle on the road must have a valid one under Rule 115 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989.

The validity periods were revised by the Central Motor Vehicles (Sixteenth Amendment) Rules, 2026, reported by All India Radio and the motoring press on 19 November 2026. Under the new schedule:

VehicleAge from first registrationPUC validity
BS-VI, private (non-transport)Up to 6 years3 years
BS-VI, private (non-transport)Over 6, up to 10 years1 year
BS-VI, private (non-transport)Over 10 years6 months
BS-VI, transport (taxis, goods vehicles)Up to 6 years2 years
BS-VI, transportOver 6, up to 10 years1 year
BS-VI, transportOver 10 years6 months
BS-IVAny6 months
BS-I, BS-II and BS-IIIAny3 months

Two details matter if you already have a certificate:

  • A certificate issued before the change keeps the validity printed on it. The new periods apply from your first renewal.
  • If you renew within ten days before expiry, the new certificate runs from the old expiry date, so you do not lose days by renewing early.

Before this change, the rule was one year for BS-IV and BS-VI vehicles and six months for others, and a new vehicle did not need its first test until a year after registration. Your car's emission standard is shown on the registration certificate and in the owner's manual; most cars sold in India since April 2020 are BS-VI. Because the rules have only just changed, check the date printed on your certificate rather than relying on memory.

Where do you get a PUC certificate, and can you carry it digitally?

You get one at an authorised PUC testing centre, often at a fuel station, and you can usually check it online and show it digitally.

  1. Drive to an authorised PUC centre. The Parivahan PUC portal lists registered centres.
  2. The operator tests the exhaust with an approved analyser while the engine runs. It takes a few minutes.
  3. You pay the fee and receive a printed certificate with a unique number, your vehicle number and the expiry date.

A Ministry of Road Transport and Highways advisory of August 2018 told states to accept documents shown through DigiLocker or mParivahan as legally equal to the originals. Whether your PUC appears in those apps depends on your state's records, so if it does not, keep the printed certificate in the car with your other papers. The Telangana Transport Department is the authority for local questions.

Driving without a valid certificate can lead to a fine, often issued as a camera or roadside e-challan. Our Hyderabad e-challan guide explains how to check and pay them.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a PUC certificate valid in India?

Under the Central Motor Vehicles (Sixteenth Amendment) Rules, 2026, reported on 19 November 2026, it depends on the emission standard and age. A private BS-VI car up to six years old gets three years, six to ten years gets one year and over ten years six months; BS-IV vehicles get six months and BS-I to BS-III three months.

Where can I get a PUC certificate in Hyderabad?

At any authorised PUC testing centre. Many are at fuel stations, and the Parivahan PUC portal has a list of registered centres. The test takes a few minutes and the certificate is issued straight away.

Can I show a digital PUC certificate?

A 2018 Ministry of Road Transport and Highways advisory says documents shown through DigiLocker or mParivahan are legally equivalent to originals. If your PUC status is not available there, carry the printed certificate.

How often should I check my tyre pressure?

At least once a month and before any long trip, with the tyres cold. Use the pressures on the sticker inside the driver's door frame or in the owner's manual, not the maximum printed on the tyre.
