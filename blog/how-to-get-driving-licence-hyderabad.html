How to get a driving licence in Hyderabad: learner's licence to car licence, step by step

How to get a driving licence in Hyderabad for a car: eligibility, documents, the learner's licence test, the 30-day wait and the RTA driving test, step by step.

To get a driving licence in Hyderabad you first get a learner's licence, practise for at least 30 days with it, then pass a practical driving test at an RTA office. The whole process starts online and takes a few weeks if you plan your slots well.

This guide walks through each step for a car (in official language, a "light motor vehicle" or LMV, non-transport). It explains what happens and in what order, but it deliberately leaves out fees and slot rules: those change, and the official websites are the only reliable source for them. Always check the Telangana Transport Department and Parivahan Sarathi before you apply.

Step 1: check you are eligible

For a private car licence you need to be at least 18 years old. You also need to be able to read and understand traffic signs and rules, because that is what the learner's licence test checks, and you must be physically fit to drive. Most applicants declare their fitness on a self-declaration form; in some cases, for example with certain medical conditions or above a certain age, a medical certificate from a registered doctor is needed. The application form tells you which applies to you.

You apply to the transport office that covers your address. Hyderabad and its surroundings are served by several Regional Transport Offices and Unit Offices, and when you book online you choose from the offices offered for your area.

Step 2: gather your documents

The exact list is shown on the application portal, and it is worth reading it before you start rather than halfway through. In general terms you will need:

  • Proof of age, such as a birth certificate, school certificate, passport or another document the portal accepts.
  • Proof of address in Telangana. If you have moved recently, make sure the address on your document matches where you are applying.
  • A recent photograph and your signature, usually uploaded in a set size and format.
  • The fitness declaration or medical certificate, as above.

Aadhaar is widely used on Parivahan Sarathi to fill in your details and, for some services, to verify your identity. Keep the phone number linked to it handy, because one-time passwords are sent to it.

Scan your documents clearly before you begin. Blurred or cropped uploads are a common reason for an application being sent back, which costs you days.

Step 3: apply for the learner's licence online

Go to Parivahan Sarathi and choose Telangana as your state, or start from the Telangana Transport Department website, which links to the same services. The usual order is:

  1. Choose the learner's licence application and fill in your personal details.
  2. Select the vehicle class. For a car this is LMV (non-transport). If you also want to ride a motorbike, you can add that class in the same application.
  3. Upload your documents, photograph and signature.
  4. Pay the fee online. The amount is shown on the portal at the time you apply.
  5. Book a slot for the learner's licence test, if your application route needs one.

Save the application number and print or download the acknowledgement. You will need it for every later step, including the driving test.

Step 4: pass the learner's licence test

The learner's licence test is a short computer-based test on traffic rules, road signs and safe driving. You read each question and choose the right answer. Depending on the current rules and the option you choose, you may take it at the transport office or online. The portal shows what is available for Telangana when you apply.

The questions are not difficult if you have prepared, but they catch people who have only skimmed the signs. Expect questions such as what a sign means, who has the right of way at a junction, and what to do when an ambulance approaches. Practice tests are available on the official Sarathi portal, and they are the best way to see the real style of question.

Spend most of your preparation time on road signs. Learn the three families (mandatory, cautionary and informatory) and the shapes that tell them apart; our guide to road signs for the learner's licence test covers them in one place.

Once you pass, you can download your learner's licence. It is valid for six months.

Step 5: practise during the waiting period

You must hold your learner's licence for at least 30 days before you can take the driving test. Treat this as training time, not waiting time. While you hold a learner's licence:

  • Drive only with a person who holds a valid licence for that class of vehicle sitting beside you.
  • Display the "L" sign on the front and back of the car, as the rules require for learners.
  • Carry your learner's licence whenever you drive.

Most people in Hyderabad take lessons from a driving school, which also provides a dual-control car. That is the safest way to learn clutch control, real braking and what it feels like when a bus pulls out in front of you. Lessons with a licensed instructor matter much more than any amount of reading.

Between lessons, a simulator can help with the thinking side of driving: keeping left, indicating early, reading signals and junctions. Step 6: book and take the driving test

After the 30 days, and before your learner's licence expires, apply for the driving licence on the same portal using your learner's licence number. Pay the test fee and book a slot at your chosen RTA office. Slots in Hyderabad can be busy, so book as soon as you are ready rather than at the last moment.

On the day, bring:

  • Your learner's licence and the application acknowledgement.
  • The original documents you uploaded, in case they are checked.
  • A suitable car with valid registration, insurance and pollution certificate, and "L" plates. Many people use their driving school's car. Check with the office whether you need to bring one and what is accepted.

The test itself checks whether you can control the car safely and follow the rules. The format depends on the office: some use dedicated test tracks with marked lanes, others may test you on a set course. Ask your driving school, or the office, what format your office uses so you can practise for it. Our RTA driving test tips explain what examiners generally look for and the mistakes that most often lead to a fail.

Do not drive alone before you hold a full driving licence. Driving on a learner's licence without a licensed person beside you is against the rules, and your insurance may not cover you.

Step 7: receive your licence

If you pass, your driving licence is issued and sent to your address, and you can usually see its status on Parivahan Sarathi. Digital copies held in DigiLocker or the mParivahan app are accepted as valid documents in India, which is useful if you leave the plastic card at home.

If you do not pass, you can apply to take the test again. The portal explains when you can rebook. Use the gap to work on whatever the examiner pointed out, and remember that your learner's licence must still be valid on the day.

A quick checklist

  1. Confirm you are 18 or older and gather proof of age and address.
  2. Apply for the learner's licence online, upload documents and pay.
  3. Prepare road signs and rules, then pass the learner's licence test.
  4. Take lessons and practise for at least 30 days with "L" plates and a licensed person beside you.
  5. Apply for the driving licence, book a test slot and take the test.
  6. Check the status and download a digital copy once it is issued.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum age for a car driving licence in Telangana?

You must be at least 18 years old to apply for a learner's licence for a car (a light motor vehicle). The same age applies across India.

How long must I wait after my learner's licence before the driving test?

You must hold the learner's licence for at least 30 days before you can take the driving test. The learner's licence stays valid for six months, so book your test within that window.

Can I apply for a learner's licence online in Hyderabad?

Yes. Applications start on Parivahan Sarathi or through the Telangana Transport Department website, where you fill in the form, upload documents, pay and book a slot. Check the official sites for which steps can be completed fully online.

Where do I take the driving test in Hyderabad?

At the Regional Transport Office or Unit Office you choose when you book your slot. Hyderabad has several offices; the Telangana Transport Department website lists them.
