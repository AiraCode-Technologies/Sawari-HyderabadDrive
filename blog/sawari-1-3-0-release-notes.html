Sawari 1.3.0: Hyderabad's famous buildings, smooth slopes and cleaner roads

Sawari 1.3.0 adds 26 famous Hyderabad places with their real names and architecture, smooths every slope, and fixes the metro, dirt and glitches on roads.

By the Sawari team·17 November 2026·2 min read
On this page
  1. Famous places, by name
  2. Smooth slopes
  3. Cleaner roads
  4. Release notes

Version 1.3.0 puts Hyderabad's best-known buildings along the routes, with their real names and an architecture modelled on the real thing. It also makes every slope a smooth, even ride and cleans up the roads.

Famous places, by name

Instead of a plain block, each of these now looks like itself and carries its name in English and Telugu:

RoutePlaces
Tank Bund and Necklace RoadDr. B.R. Ambedkar Telangana State Secretariat, the Dr. B.R. Ambedkar statue, the Telangana Martyrs' Memorial, the Buddha statue, Birla Mandir, Prasads IMAX, Jalavihar, Lumbini Park, NTR Gardens, Sanjeevaiah Park
CharminarMecca Masjid, Chowmahalla Palace, Salar Jung Museum, the Telangana High Court, Osmania General Hospital
HITEC CityCyber Towers, Inorbit Mall, Cyber Gateway, TCS Deccan Park, ITC Kohenur, Trident, Peddamma Temple
Banjara HillsGVK One, City Center Mall, Taj Krishna, Taj Deccan

They stand on their real footprints from OpenStreetMap, are solid to drive into, and sit level on sloping ground. Read how we build the city for more on the map data.

Smooth slopes

Roads used to follow every bump in the terrain data, and stepped up or down where they met another road. They are now graded into even slopes that ride smoothly. On Banjara Hills the sharp changes of grade fell from 12 to 3, and on HITEC City from 20 to 8.

Cleaner roads

  • No more dirt showing through the asphalt: the ground now stays beneath every road, including beside flyovers.
  • One metro viaduct and one row of pillars per line. The map draws each track separately, which used to double them up.
  • Underpasses no longer lie on top of the roads above them, and the Telugu Talli Flyover now rises clear of Lower Tank Bund Road.
  • A road no longer gets a parapet instead of a footpath just because the ground beside it dips.

Release notes

From this version on, every update gets a post like this one; the version number in the site footer links here. Drive the Old City to see Mecca Masjid and Chowmahalla Palace.

Sawari is a practice simulator. It does not replace lessons with a licensed instructor or the official RTA test. Always follow the signs, signals and traffic police on the real road.
