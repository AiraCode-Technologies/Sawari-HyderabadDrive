What is Sawari? The free Hyderabad driving simulator, in key facts

What Sawari is, in key facts: a free browser driving simulator on five real Hyderabad routes, on phones, tablets and PCs, built from OpenStreetMap data.

By the Sawari team at AiraCode Technologies·23 November 2026·5 min read

Sawari is a free 3D driving simulator that runs in a web browser and puts you on five real Hyderabad roads, from Tank Bund to the Charminar and over the Durgam Cheruvu cable bridge. It plays on phones, tablets, laptops and PCs, scores every drive and explains each mistake, so new drivers can practise Hyderabad traffic before they meet it.

Who makes Sawari?

Sawari is made by AiraCode Technologies. New versions come out often, and the release notes list what changed in each one.

What does the name mean?

Sawari is the Urdu word for a ride, and for a royal procession through the old city. It suits a simulator that takes you through Hyderabad's streets, from the Qutb Shahi Old City to the HITEC City flyovers.

Which roads can you drive?

Five routes, chosen to go from easy to hard:

  • Tank Bund Road (1.7 km, easy): the lakeside road along Hussain Sagar, with no turns. See the Tank Bund Road driving guide.
  • Necklace Road (5.0 km, easy): the loop round the west and north shores of the lake. See the Necklace Road guide.
  • Cyber Towers to Jubilee Hills (7.4 km, medium): HITEC City Road, Inorbit Mall Road, the Durgam Cheruvu cable bridge, Road No. 45 and Road No. 36. See the HITEC City drive.
  • Banjara Hills Road No. 1 (2.6 km, medium): the climb from Masab Tank through busy junctions. See the Banjara Hills guide.
  • Charminar and Pathergatti (2.1 km, hard): Old City streets round the Charminar, with nine turns. See the Charminar guide.

How realistic is it?

The road layouts, lane counts, one-way streets, flyovers, buildings, lakes, signals and places of worship come from OpenStreetMap, and the ground follows real elevation data, so slopes such as the climb up Banjara Hills Road No. 1 are real. The car has tyre grip, weight transfer, an automatic gearbox and ABS. Some things are estimated where the map has no detail: speed breakers, U-turn openings, roadside stalls, parked vehicles, hoardings and most street lamps. The behind-the-build article explains what is mapped and what is estimated.

What does a drive check?

Each drive is marked out of 100. The debrief lists every fault with the time it happened and advice on avoiding it:

  • Speeding over the limit for the road
  • Driving on the wrong side of the centre line
  • Going the wrong way on a one-way road
  • Leaving the road
  • Running a red light
  • Collisions with vehicles, pedestrians, the median or walls
  • Taking a speed breaker too fast
  • Turning without an indicator

Spoken turn-by-turn directions guide you, in the most natural English voice your device has.

Who is it for?

Mainly learner and new drivers in Hyderabad who want to get used to left-hand traffic, signals, indicators and local traffic before driving for real, and people who already drive but want to try an unfamiliar part of the city. It is also simply a free way to drive round Hyderabad's landmarks. Read how to make the most of it in driving in Hyderabad for beginners.

Can you share your score?

Yes. After each drive you can share a score card picture made for WhatsApp status and Instagram Stories, or send a friend a link that challenges them to beat your score on the same route, at the same time of day and with the same traffic.

What Sawari is not

It is not a substitute for lessons with a licensed instructor or for the RTA driving test, and it is not affiliated with the Telangana Transport Department or the traffic police. It covers five routes, not the whole city, and it has no mirrors, no weather and no multiplayer.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sawari free?

Yes. Sawari is free to play in a web browser, with no download, no app to install and no sign-up.

Does Sawari work on mobile?

Yes. Since version 1.6 Sawari runs on phones and tablets held sideways, with an on-screen steering wheel and GO and BRAKE pedals. On laptops and desktops you drive with a keyboard or a gamepad.

Are Sawari's routes the real roads of Hyderabad?

Yes. The five routes are built from OpenStreetMap road data and open elevation data, so the layouts, lanes, flyovers and gradients follow the real roads. Some details, such as shop stalls, speed breakers and most street lamps, are placed by rule where the map has no detail.

Can Sawari replace driving lessons or the RTA test?

No. Sawari is a practice simulator. It helps you learn habits such as keeping left and signalling, but it does not replace lessons with a licensed instructor or the official test, and it is not affiliated with the Telangana Transport Department.
